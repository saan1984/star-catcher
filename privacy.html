Star Catcher – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026

Star Catcher ("the game") is a casual arcade game published by saan1984 ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game does and does not collect.

Summary

The game does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no tracking.

Information we collect

None. We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera, and the game does not request any of those permissions.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your personal best score in your device's local storage so it can show it next time you play. This stays on your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game removes it.

Third parties

The game does not include third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software, and does not send data to third parties.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/saan1984/star-catcher/issues.
